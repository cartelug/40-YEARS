The Provenance Ledger

Sources & methodology

A record is only as strong as its evidence. Every figure on the record traces to a public statistical series; every image is accounted for. Where a figure is fixed by editorial decision, that decision is stated.

Methodology

The record uses five approved figures as its spine — GDP per capita $330 → $986 · electricity 60 → 2,048 MW · poverty 56% → 16% · 1.8M refugees hosted · 40 years of stability. These were fixed for the anniversary edition after reconciliation between national statistics (UBOS, ERA, OPM) and international series (World Bank, UNHCR), and they do not change between sections. Supporting facts in individual chapters are contextual and cited below. Baselines refer to 1986 unless a series begins later (poverty: 1992/93 survey round, the earliest comparable national line).

Corrections: the record is a living archive through the anniversary year. Verified corrections are entered with a dated note in this ledger.

The ledger

GDP per capita: $330 → $986

Gross domestic product per person, current US dollars, 1986 baseline against the most recent full-year figure.

Provenance

Uganda Bureau of Statistics national accounts; World Bank World Development Indicators (NY.GDP.PCAP.CD).

Basis

The 1986 figure reflects the post-conflict trough; the trajectory holds across both national and Bretton-Woods series. Approved figure fixed for this record.

Electricity generated: 60 MW → 2,048 MW

Installed national generation capacity: one ageing dam (Nalubaale) in 1986 against the grid of 2024–26 including Karuma (600 MW), Isimba (183 MW), Bujagali (250 MW) and grid solar.

Provenance

Electricity Regulatory Authority (ERA) installed-capacity statistics; Ministry of Energy and Mineral Development.

Basis

Capacity, not dispatch. The 2,048 MW milestone follows the commissioning of Karuma. Approved figure fixed for this record.

Poverty: 56% → 16%

Share of the population below the national poverty line, early-1990s baseline against the most recent Uganda National Household Survey round.

Provenance

Uganda Bureau of Statistics — Uganda National Household Survey series (1992/93 baseline of 56.4%).

Basis

Measured against the national line, not the international $2.15 line. Approved figure fixed for this record.

Refugees hosted: 1.8M

Refugees and asylum-seekers hosted on Ugandan territory — the largest refugee population in Africa.

Provenance

UNHCR Uganda operational data portal; Office of the Prime Minister (Department of Refugees).

Basis

Under the Refugee Act 2006: land allocation, freedom of movement, right to work. Approved figure fixed for this record.

Stability: 40 years

Unbroken constitutional order from January 1986 to 2026, after a quarter-century marked by coups and civil war.

Provenance

Constitution of the Republic of Uganda (1995); national historical record.

Basis

The anniversary this record marks. Approved figure fixed for this record.

Supporting facts (chapters 04–09)

Literacy 43% → 70%+; ~5,000 factories from 80; HIV prevalence −70% through the 1990s; 51% household electricity access; 51 km Kampala–Entebbe Expressway; ~230,000 barrels/day oil at peak; Bidi Bidi and Nakivale settlements.

Provenance

UBOS statistical abstracts; Ministry of Health HIV estimates (peak ~15% c.1991 to ~5% by 2001); UNRA project records; Petroleum Authority of Uganda projections; UNHCR settlement profiles.

Basis

Contextual figures supporting the five approved figures; each traces to the cited public series.

Imagery

Thirteen landscape photographs of Uganda (Lake Bunyonyi, Sipi Falls, Rwenzori, Bwindi, Mount Elgon, Queen Elizabeth NP, Murchison Falls, Kigezi terraces, Lake Victoria at Jinja); twenty-five illustrative plates (hero portrait fusion, topographic, ledger and ceremonial surfaces) generated for this record.

Provenance

Photography: supplied production archive — rights to be confirmed with the Presidential Press Unit before public launch. Illustrative plates: commissioned AI-assisted generations, treated as artwork, not documentary evidence.

Basis

Two generated plates were rejected at intake (wrong national geography; degenerate output) and do not appear. All geography used for data display is drawn from surveyed coordinates, not generated art.

Typography

TeX Gyre Heros & TeX Gyre Heros Cn (GUST e-foundry), self-hosted subsets — the URW Nimbus Sans lineage of the Helvetica design.

Provenance

GUST Font Licence (free to embed and distribute). Platform Helvetica Neue is used where the operating system provides it.

Basis

No commercial font files are distributed with this site.
