## 04 · Achievements (tabbed sector explorer)
- Kicker: **04 · The record**
- H2: **Achievements across every sector**
- Standfirst: Eight fronts of national transformation. Select a sector to explore the evidence.

### 01 · Peace & Stability — "The foundation of everything"
- After the Amin and Obote years, the NRM brought an end to the cycle of coups and civil war that had defined the country.
- Four decades of relative stability became the precondition for every other gain — investment, schooling, health and growth all rest on it.
- Tag pills: 1986 turning point · End of the coup cycle · National unity
- Stats: **40** years of stability · **1986** the turning point
- Big visual: **40** — years of unbroken peace after decades of upheaval

### 02 · Economy — "From collapse to consistent growth"
- The new government inherited a shattered economy, then launched an IMF recovery program, reformed the currency and built institutions like the Uganda Revenue Authority.
- The result is one of Africa's steadier growth stories: real income per person has roughly tripled and the economy has diversified far beyond subsistence farming.
- Tag pills: IMF recovery 1987 · Currency reform · ~6.5% avg growth
- Stats: **3×** real income / person · **16%** poverty, from 56% · **~6.5%** avg annual growth
- Chart axis: 1986 · $330 → 2024 · $986 (data points: 330, 360, 400, 470, 560, 650, 760, 860, 986)
- Chart caption: A **3× rise** in real income, sustained across four decades of rapid population growth.

### 03 · Energy — "The lights came on"
- From a single ageing dam generating 60 megawatts, four major hydropower plants on the Nile — led by the 600 MW Karuma and 183 MW Isimba — plus solar have lifted capacity past 2,000 MW.
- Where half of district headquarters had power in 1986, almost every district is now connected — and the country has moved from blackouts to a surplus it can export.
- Tag pills: Karuma · 600 MW · Isimba · 183 MW · Deficit → surplus
- Stats: **2,048** MW, from 60 · **51%** access, from ~12% · **600 MW** Karuma Dam
- Bar chart: 1986 = 60 · 2007 = ~600 · 2019 = 1,177 · 2024 = 2,048
- Chart caption: Installed generation capacity, megawatts — a **34× rise** in four decades.

### 04 · Health — "The HIV turnaround the world studied"
- Uganda was the first country in sub-Saharan Africa to confront HIV/AIDS openly, mobilising whole communities while others stayed silent.
- Prevalence fell by around 70% through the 1990s — from a peak near 15% to roughly 5% — a decline researchers compared to a highly effective vaccine, and the global model for prevention.
- Tag pills: First to act · The "ABC" model · A global blueprint
- Stats: **~70%** fall in prevalence · **1st** in region to act · **~5%** from a 15% peak
- Chart axis: 1986 · 1991 · ~15% · 2001 · ~5%
- Chart caption: Rising to a peak around **1991**, then one of the sharpest sustained declines on record.

### 05 · Education — "A classroom for every child"
- The 1997 introduction of Universal Primary Education threw open the school gates, driving enrolment toward universal levels and lifting adult literacy from 43% to over 70%.
- Universal Secondary Education followed, and a handful of institutions grew into a national network of universities — a whole generation given the chance to learn.
- Tag pills: UPE · 1997 · Universal Secondary · Many new universities
- Stats: **1997** Universal Primary Ed. · **70%+** literacy, from 43%
- Bar chart: 1986 = 43% · Today = 70%+
- Chart caption: Adult literacy rate — near-universal primary enrolment is the engine of every other gain.

### 06 · Infrastructure — "Roads, bridges and wings"
- The Kampala–Entebbe Expressway, new bridges across the Nile, thousands of kilometres of tarmac and the revival of Uganda Airlines have reconnected the country and its neighbours.
- Modern infrastructure turned isolated districts into part of a national and regional economy.
- Tag pills: Entebbe Expressway · New Nile bridges · Uganda Airlines revived
- Stats: **51 km** Entebbe Expressway · **Revived** national airline
- Image slot caption: Photograph · the Entebbe Expressway, a new bridge or Uganda Airlines

### 07 · Agriculture & Industry — "A productive nation"
- Factories grew from around 80 in 1986 to thousands today, with value addition in coffee, dairy and grain turning raw produce into export earnings.
- Coffee — the country's signature crop — remains one of Africa's largest exports, now increasingly processed at home.
- Tag pills: Value addition · Coffee & dairy · Manufacturing growth
- Stats: **~5,000** factories, from 80 · **Top** coffee exporter in Africa
- Bar chart: 1986 = 80 · 2010 = ~1,200 · 2019 = ~5,000
- Chart caption: Factories operating — a roughly **60-fold** rise in industrial capacity.

### 08 · Humanity — "Africa's most generous open door"
- Uganda hosts around 1.8 million refugees — more than any other African country — under a 2006 law widely regarded as one of the most progressive in the world.
- Rather than camps behind fences, refugees receive a plot of land, freedom of movement and the right to work and farm. The world calls it simply "the Uganda Model."
- Tag pills: 1.8M hosted · Land + the right to work · Bidi Bidi · Nakivale
- Stats: **1.8M** refugees hosted · **#1** host in Africa · **2006** landmark Refugee Act
- Big visual: **1.8M** — refugees from across the region living safely in Uganda — Africa's #1 host nation

- Kicker: **05 · Across the nation**

### Central Region — Kampala · Wakiso · Mukono · Masaka
- Stats: **#1** industrial hub · **1,200+** new water links
- The Kampala–Entebbe Expressway, the flagship modern highway
- The densest factory belt and industrial parks in the nation
- Parish Development Model rolled out across its districts
- Piped-water extensions reaching fast-growing suburbs

### Eastern Region — Jinja · Mbale · Soroti · Tororo
- Stats: **2** dams on the Nile · **50 MW** grid solar
- Isimba and Bujagali hydropower stations on the Victoria Nile
- Soroti and Tororo solar plants feeding the national grid
- Grain and produce value-addition mills for cooperatives
- Jinja's revival as an industrial and tourism centre

### Northern Region — Gulu · Lira · Arua · Moroto
- Stats: **600 MW** Karuma Dam · **#1** recovery story
- The 600 MW Karuma Dam — the nation's largest power plant
- Recovery and rebuilding across the post-conflict north
- West Nile electrification reaching long-isolated districts
- Home to Bidi Bidi, among the world's largest settlements

### Western Region — Mbarara · Hoima · Fort Portal · Kabale
- Stats: **230k** barrels/day peak · **#1** oil region
- The Lake Albert oilfields and the nation's energy future
- A thriving dairy and agriculture economy
- Emyooga and SACCO enterprise support for traders
- Gateway to the western tourism circuit
