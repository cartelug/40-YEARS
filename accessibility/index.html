Accessibility & languages

A national record belongs to everyone it records. This site is built to WCAG 2.2 AA, tested with keyboard and screen reader, and engineered for the phones and networks Ugandans actually use.

What we built in

  • Full keyboard access: chapter navigation, the regional map, and the archive filmstrip all operate with Tab, Enter and the arrow keys — with a visible 2px gold focus indicator throughout.
  • Reduced motion honoured completely: with “reduce motion” set, the spine appears fully drawn, figures show final values, and all parallax, pinning and Ken Burns effects are disabled.
  • Contrast at AA or better on every text/background pair, in both the navy and cream chapters.
  • Alternative text on every informative image; decorative plates are hidden from assistive technology.
  • One heading hierarchy, landmark regions, and a skip link to the main content.
  • Touch targets of at least 24px; no interaction depends on hover.
  • Light pages by design: the record loads on a low-end Android phone over a slow connection.
