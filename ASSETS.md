## 3 · Optimisation

- **Formats:** AVIF q50 (photos on dense sources tuned lower) + WebP q72 + progressive mozJPEG q78 fallback.
- **Widths:** desktop plates 480/960/1440/1672(native); mobile plates 540/941(native); photos 480/960/1440/1920 (dense-foliage sources capped at 1200).
- **Metadata stripped** on every encode; **LQIP** 24-px WebP data-URI per image, inlined via manifest.
- **Manifest:** `site/src/data/images.json` — consumed by `<Pic/>` which renders `<picture>` with `srcset/sizes`, art-direction mobile twin at <768px, `loading=lazy`, `decoding=async`, explicit `width/height` (zero CLS).
- **Budgets held:** hero AVIF 57 KB at 1672 (<120 KB budget); every photo ≤90 KB at its served width (two dense sources noted at 107 KB max rung, capped at 1200 px); full image set 14 MB across 3 formats × all rungs (only one format/rung is ever downloaded).
